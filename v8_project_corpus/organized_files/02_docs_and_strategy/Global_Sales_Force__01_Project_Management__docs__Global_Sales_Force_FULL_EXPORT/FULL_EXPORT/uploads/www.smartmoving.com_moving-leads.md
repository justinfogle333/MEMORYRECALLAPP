## Repeat business

Moving company marketing can be a hard nut to crack. But the easiest lead to close? The one that already knows you. Top movers get 43% of their revenue from [past customers and referrals](https://www.smartmoving.com/blog/moving-referral-program?hsLang=en).

**✅ Turn one-time customers into lifelong fans:**

- Create a loyalty program
- Set up automated emails (that don't sound like robots)
- Call out key details to show you remember them
- Make it ridiculously easy to book again 😉

**💯 Pro tip:** Use your CRM to automate follow-ups without losing the personal touch. Send birthday cards, move anniversaries, and seasonal check-ins. And make sure every message adds value—nobody wants another "just checking in" email.

## Google ads (PPC)

Here's why 61% of successful movers swear by Google Ads: when someone types "movers near me" into Google, they're ready to book—not just browse. But there's a catch.

[PPC can get pricey](https://www.smartmoving.com/blog/google-ads-are-eating-your-budget?hsLang=en). The average mover burns through $6,300 monthly on [Google Ads](https://www.smartmoving.com/blog/how-much-should-you-spend-on-google-ads-for-your-moving-business?hsLang=en). Let's make sure that money comes back with profit.

**✅ Get more from your ad budget:**

- Target "ready-to-book" [moving company keywords](https://www.smartmoving.com/blog/keywords-for-moving-company?hsLang=en) (think "movers near me")
- Cut zip codes that don't convert after 50 clicks
- Go hyperlocal in high-income areas
- Block budget-draining keywords like "free" and "cheap"
- Split campaigns by service type (local moves ≠ storage)

**💯 Pro tip:** Start small in your best areas. Use landing pages to track your most effective ads, then scale what works.

## Direct mail

Everyone's going digital. That's exactly why a well-designed [moving company postcard](https://www.smartmoving.com/blog/moving-company-postcards?hsLang=en) hits different. Sure, conversion rates aren't massive, but it doesn’t take much to recoup your costs.

**✅ Make your mailers work:**

- Include a clear call-to-action (like "Call Now!")
- Include multiple contact methods (phone, email, website)
- Track using unique phone numbers
- Follow up fast when those calls come in

**💯 Pro tip:**Hit them twice—mail plus digital retargeting. Double the touchpoints, double the results.

## Facebook ads

24% of profitable movers are [crushing it on Facebook](https://www.smartmoving.com/blog/moving-leads-facebook?hsLang=en) because the targeting on this platform is top notch. New job? Getting married? Just listed a house? These people need movers—and Facebook knows exactly who they are.

**✅ Cash in on Facebook ads**

- Target life events (new job, engagement, etc.)
- Build custom audiences
- Retarget warm leads
- Test different ad formats
- Focus on high-converting zip codes

**💯 Pro tip:** Create a "moving soon" audience from people browsing real estate sites. Use lead forms to grab their info right on Facebook—no clicking away required.

## Reviews and reputation

Here's a truth bomb: 95% of movers have decent ratings, but 82% have fewer than 500 reviews. When someone's trusting you with everything they own, they're picking the mover with 500 five-star reviews over the one with 50—even if both are great companies.

**✅ Turn good work into great marketing:**

- [Ask for reviews](https://www.smartmoving.com/blog/moving-company-customer-reviews?hsLang=en) immediately
- Respond publicly to positive *and* negative reviews
- Post those 5-star reviews everywhere
- Watch for trends in your review sentiment

**💯 Pro tip:** Set up [automated review requests](https://help.smartmoving.com/en/articles/7861187-automatically-send-a-move-review) that go out right after the move—while you're still their hero. Bonus points if your system flags low ratings so you can fix issues fast.

## SEO

Want free leads while you sleep? That's [moving company SEO](https://www.smartmoving.com/moving-company-seo?hsLang=en). 70% of people pick their mover straight from Google Maps. And these leads [convert at 14.6%](https://digitaldelane.com/small-business-marketing-strategies-by-google-certified-digital-marketer#:~:text=By%20leveraging%20their%20expertise%20and,close%20rate%20for%20outbound%20leads.)—way better than the 1.7% you get from traditional marketing.

**✅ Nail these SEO basics:**

- Set up and optimize your [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/us/business-profile/) (like, yesterday)
- Use free tools like Brightlocal to find keywords
- Build local landing pages
- Get more reviews (goal = at least 33% of jobs)
- Keep your NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistent

**💯 Pro tip:**Start with your Google Business Profile. It's free, takes 20 minutes, and brings in more leads than most paid ads.

## Content marketing

Know what your local competitors aren't doing? Creating content that actually helps people move, like neighborhood guides, cost comparisons, and insider tips from moving experts.

This stuff [brings in leads](https://www.smartmoving.com/blog/moving-company-content-marketing?hsLang=en) for years, not just during your next ad campaign.

**✅ Create content that cuts through:**

- Create local moving guides that help vs. sell
- Answer real customer questions in your moving blog
- Mix up your formats (blog posts, videos, checklists)
- Make it easy to contact you from every piece of content

**💯 Pro tip:** Create one killer city guide, then slice it into social posts, emails, and video tips. One piece of content, dozens of uses.

## Social media marketing

Think social media's just for sharing memes? Think again. In 2025, Instagram and Facebook are lead machines for media-savvy movers.

**✅ Sharpen your social brand:**

- Post consistently (a calendar helps)
- Spotlight happy customers in your [social media posts](https://www.smartmoving.com/blog/social-media-post-ideas-moving-companies?hsLang=en)
- Have a sense of humor (it shows you’re human)
- Make it easy to book from your social profiles

**💯 Pro tip:** Offer a small reward when customers share their move on social and tag you. More reviews, more leads—just remember to [follow FTC rules](https://www.dubofflaw.com/ftc-guidelines-for-endorsements-%EF%BB%BF%EF%BB%BFand-testimonials/) about disclosing incentives.
