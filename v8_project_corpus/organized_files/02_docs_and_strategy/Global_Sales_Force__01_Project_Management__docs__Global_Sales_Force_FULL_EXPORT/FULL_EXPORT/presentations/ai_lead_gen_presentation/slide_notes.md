While the goal of influencing AI through community platforms is correct, we need to be extremely careful about how we approach it. Astroturfing, or posting fake questions and answers on Reddit, now carries severe legal risks. The FTC's new Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule, effective October 21, 2024, explicitly prohibits creating or disseminating fake reviews. The penalties are massive, reaching $53,088 per violation, and the FTC has already started enforcement actions. Getting caught would be catastrophic for us, especially with 14 brands under one umbrella; one scandal could damage our entire portfolio. Reddit also actively detects and bans astroturfing accounts, and over 63% of branded threads are already negative. This is why we must protect our portfolio by doing this the right way.
