The New Paradigm

GEO Is the Legitimate, Scalable Way to Win AI Recommendations

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of structuring a brand's digital presence so that AI-powered search platforms can retrieve, cite, and recommend it. Think of it as SEO for the AI era.
GEO is now a recognized discipline with dedicated conferences, agency specializations, and purpose-built tools. This is not experimental — it's mainstream.
